# Idea Catcher 🎙️ → 🧠

*A free, self-hosted assistant that turns every voice note or message into an organised Notion idea — automatically. By [Zero To Reel](https://zerotoreel.com) · @theaikat.*

## What it does
Send any idea to your private Telegram bot — **speak it or type it**. Every hour a free job in the cloud:
- transcribes voice notes with local AI (Whisper),
- sorts each idea by a keyword you choose,
- files it into your Notion database.

Your computer can be off. It stays on the free tier. **No paid APIs.**

## Get it (≈2 minutes)
1. Open **[SETUP.md](SETUP.md)** and copy the whole file.
2. Paste it to **Claude** and say: *"Please set this up for me."*
3. Answer a couple of simple questions (your folders + keywords). Claude builds the rest.

## What's inside
- **SETUP.md** — the builder you give to Claude.
- **docs/simple-guide.md** — plain-English how-to.
- **docs/how-it-works.md** — the detailed explanation.
- **templates/** — the engine Claude drops into your own private repo.

## Free & private
Local Whisper (no paid transcription), GitHub's free Actions minutes, free Notion, free Telegram. A built-in cost guard warns you and stops before you could ever be billed. Your bot only listens to you; your tokens live in GitHub's encrypted secrets.
