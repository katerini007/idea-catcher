# Your Idea Catcher — the simple version

## What it is
A little assistant that lives in your phone, inside Telegram. Whenever you have an idea — a reel
concept, a to-do, a note about a client — you just send it to your assistant: **type it, or hold
the mic and talk.** It writes it down, understands your voice notes, sorts it into the right folder,
and drops it into your Notion — automatically, even while you sleep. **You never lose an idea again.**

## Why you'll love it
- **Catch ideas the second they happen** — by voice, no typing, no opening five apps.
- **It sorts them for you** (Business, Personal, Admin… whatever folders you chose).
- **Everything lands in one tidy Notion list** you can act on later.
- **It runs by itself in the background.** Your computer can be off. It's **free**.

## How to use it (your daily habit)
1. Open the chat with your assistant in Telegram.
2. **Say a keyword first, then your idea.** For example:
   - *"BUSINESS — a reel about how I plan my week"*
   - *"ADMIN — renew the domain next month"*
   - *"AGENDA — call the accountant Thursday"*
   - or just send the idea with **no keyword** — it goes to your **Inbox** to sort later.
3. That's it. **Within the hour** it appears — written out and sorted — in your Notion.

## One tip
Say the keyword **clearly at the very start.** That's how it knows which folder the idea belongs to.
Everything else — deciding what's important, what to do next — you (or your assistant, in a chat)
handle whenever you like.
