Your best ideas show up at the worst times.

In the shower. Mid-walk. Right before sleep. By the time you're at your desk, they're gone. Idea Catcher lets you catch them the second they happen — just talk.

1

Speak or type it

Send your idea to your private assistant in Telegram. Hold the mic and talk, or type a line. Two seconds, done.

2

It sorts it for you

Voice notes get transcribed. Each idea is filed under the folder you chose — Business, Personal, Admin, whatever you set.

3

Lands in Notion

Within the hour it appears, written out and organised, in one tidy list you can act on later.

You're in control of the folders.

Start a note with a keyword and it files itself. No keyword? It waits in your Inbox to sort later.

BUSINESS —a reel about how I plan my week
ADMIN —renew the domain next month
AGENDA —call the accountant on Thursday
(no keyword) —goes straight to your Inbox
